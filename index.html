Hand of History

A medieval life simulation where you begin as nobody.

Every person in the village lives a life of their own: they work, eat, argue, make friends, fall in love and remember what you did. You control only yourself. Become a smith, a farmer, a soldier or an outlaw — or earn enough trust to lead the village one day. Prefer to guide everyone at once? Play it as a colony sim instead.

  • A world that lives without you. Villages, lords and bandits go on whether you are watching or not.
  • Start with nothing. Find a place, build a shelter, survive, meet people.
  • Every person has a story. Needs, traits, friendships and grudges shape what happens next.
